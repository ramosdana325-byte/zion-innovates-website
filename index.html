Our Story

We didn't start as a technology solutions company. But this is where we choose to stay.

From 2021 to 2023, we operated as a telco subcontractor for Telecom Corporation, handling SLI and OSP projects across North and Central Luzon.

Our teams were deployed in multiple provinces, managed large workforces, and responded to critical situations, including disaster recovery operations to restore communications left severely wounded by Super Typhoon Odette and Super Typhoon Karding.

2021

Learning in Uncertainty

In the middle of the pandemic, we began as a subcontractor under Jedlian Cavite, working as an SLI subcontractor.

Operating under strict limitations, heightened risk conditions required discipline, adaptability, and coordination, values that continue to define how we work today.

2022

Scaling Under Pressure

At the height of our telco work, we were deploying 100+ personnel across 11 provinces in North and Central Luzon.

During this period, nearly half of our workforce was mobilized to the Visayas, specifically Cebu, as a rescue subcontractor following the devastation of Typhoon Odette.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

How are your quotations prepared?

Quotations are based on an initial site assessment, defined scope, and system design. We provide clear and itemized costing so clients understand exactly what they are paying for and why, before any work begins.

Can changes be made during implementation?

Yes. We provide continuous updates and coordinate adjustments when site conditions or client requirements change. Any changes are subject to scope review and approval. Price adjustments may apply for approved variations, but changes that alter the entire project scope are treated as a separate discussion.

Do we need to have a system design already?

No. You may come with a concept or none at all. We can develop the design from scratch or refine an existing plan into a buildable, efficient solution aligned with your operations.

Who is responsible for system access and credentials?

System access and credentials are transferred to the client upon completion. Credential handling and security are discussed during turnover.

How do you manage cable routing and concealment for Auxiliary Systems?

Cable routing is planned during design and coordinated with architectural and structural elements. We provide rough-ins and wiring-ins where applicable to minimize exposed cables and maintain a clean installation.

Who owns the system after installation and/or implementation?

You own your system, no lock-ins. We do not hold systems hostage through restricted access or proprietary control. After turnover, system access and credentials belong to you.
